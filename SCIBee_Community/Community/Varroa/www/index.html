Varroa Mite Data Point

For selected location on map.

  • Presence detected: Varroa mites were detected in the colony, but the infestation level is below the recommended treatment threshold.
  • Infestation detected: Varroa mite levels have reached or exceeded the recommended treatment threshold (typically around 2–3% of sampled bees, depending on the season and local beekeeping guidelines). Treatment is recommended.
  • Formic acid treatment: A treatment using formic acid to reduce Varroa mite populations. Effective against mites on both adult bees and within capped brood.
  • Oxalic acid treatment: A treatment using oxalic acid, most effective when little or no brood is present, as it primarily targets mites on adult bees.
  • Commercial miticide treatment: Treatment using an approved commercial miticide product (e.g., strips or other registered formulations) according to the manufacturer's instructions.
  • Quantity: Number of beehive colonies affected by reported action.
