Our mission

Mechatronic support for smarter beekeeping.

We develop mechatronic support systems for automated beehive information gathering and interpretation.

Our goal is to connect practical beekeeping experience, scientific research and technological expertise through modular solutions that can be adapted to specific needs.

  • ✓ Gather useful information from beehives
  • ✓ Support interpretation of collected information
  • ✓ Develop adaptable, modular systems

In the field

From development to real-world testing.

SCIBee is in the pilot and testing stage. Field work helps us explore practical use, evaluate system behaviour and identify improvements for future development.

We never stop improving—design, test, deploy at scale, analyse, and repeat, turning every lesson learned into the next step forward.
